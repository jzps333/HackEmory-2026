# Research agent

Long, hands-off research over Chert. Say "research NASDAQ trading strategies" on FaceTime (or text
`/research ...`) and hang up. The agent plans, searches (Gemini with Google Search), reads, takes
notes and decides what is still missing, on its own, for up to 15 minutes. If it needs a decision
it texts you; when it's done it FaceTimes you back and explains the findings in depth, stopping to
ask what you want to dig into, with section cards and sources on the video.

- **Runs on:** our server. **Tools:** speak, screen, memory, research (verified plugins only).
- **Guardrails:** 15-minute cap, read-only (no logins, purchases or posting), every source listed.
