# Tutor

Learn anything on a FaceTime call, with the notes written on your screen as you go. Say "help me
learn derivatives" (or "teach me", "explain this", "tutor").

How a lesson goes:

1. It asks what you already know about the topic and skips that.
2. A notebook page appears on the call's video with the topic as its title.
3. Each step is handwritten on the page one at a time (it writes itself, with a pencil), then
   explained out loud in a sentence or two. The step being explained is highlighted; earlier steps
   stay above it and scroll up when the page is full.
4. After every step it checks in ("Does that make sense, or is anything tripping you up?") and
   waits. Say you're stuck, or "why are we doing this?", and it asks which part, explains the why a
   different way and sticks a yellow side note under the step.
5. When a picture helps (a function, a slope), it draws a small graph under the step: axes, the
   curve, and a dot with its tangent line and slope.
6. At the end it writes a recap step and offers to save the notes to your history. Nothing is
   saved unless you say yes.

Math is written in plain text with Unicode (x², √x, ∫, π, ≤, →), so it reads cleanly on a phone.
The hand is Caveat (SIL Open Font License); symbols it lacks are drawn in DejaVu Sans.

- **Runs on:** our server, on the call. **Tools:** speak, screen, whiteboard, memory.
- The notes are drawn as text on the agent's own video; nothing on the page can be tapped or run.
