# Squat coach

A live coaching plugin with memory: prop your phone up, FaceTime Chert, and squat.

- **During the call:** Gemini watches the video and gives one short cue per rep ("go deeper",
  "knees out", "chest up"); the cue appears on the video Chert sends back.
- **After the call:** a two-sentence analysis by text, with a link to your history.
- **Over time:** every session is saved to your private history (My apps, sign in with your phone).

- **Runs on:** our server, on the call. **Tools:** camera, speak, screen, memory.
- **Limit:** the model sees about one frame per second, so cues are coaching, not measurement.
