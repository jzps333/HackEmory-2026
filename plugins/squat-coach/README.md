# Squat coach

A live coaching plugin: prop your phone up, FaceTime Chert, and squat. Gemini watches the video and
gives one short cue per rep ("go deeper", "knees out", "chest up"), and the cue appears on the
video Chert sends back.

- **Runs on:** our server, on the call.
- **Tools:** camera, speak, screen, memory.
- **Limit:** the model sees about one frame per second, so cues are coaching, not measurement.
