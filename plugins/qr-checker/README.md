# QR checker (ScanSafe)

The first community plugin. Point your phone's FaceTime camera at a QR code (parking meter, menu,
flyer) and Chert opens the link in a locked-down browser on our server while you watch it live,
scores it for scams and tells you in plain words whether it's safe.

- **Runs on:** our server; links open in the locked-down VM (never on your phone).
- **Tools:** camera, speak, screen, scam check, safe browser.
- **Made for:** Security track. Built by the ScanSafe team at EmoryHacks 2026.
