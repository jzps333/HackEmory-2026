# Study buddy

Spaced repetition over FaceTime. Add cards by voice ("add a card: mitochondria, the powerhouse of
the cell"), then say "quiz me". Cards you get right come back in a day, then 2.5x later each time;
cards you miss come back in a minute. Your deck and what's due are on My apps (sign in with your
phone number).

- **Runs on:** our server, on the call. **Tools:** speak, screen, memory, flashcards.
